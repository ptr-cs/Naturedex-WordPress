## A familiar feeling. A real wild world.

Naturedex is a field guide inspired by the curiosity and pixel-filled adventures of handheld games. Its creatures are real Virginia wildlife, and its illustrations are original.

## How to read this guide

The 24 entries are a curated starting collection across six animal groups, not a complete checklist of Virginia wildlife. Size measurements describe general adult ranges where a source supports them. Reference links are provided inside each entry.

The map’s regions are broad habitat guides. Pins mark regions, not animal observations or precise distribution boundaries. They are intended to help you explore the collection.

## Where your notes live

Published field notes and species entries are stored in WordPress. Your discovery collection and sprite motion preference are saved only in this browser; they are not sent to the site or synchronized between devices.

## Accessible exploration

Every map region has a labeled button. You can explore the complete guide as a list, use the interface with a keyboard, and reduce motion through your device preferences. Automated testing supports this work; manual accessibility evaluation remains an ongoing task.
